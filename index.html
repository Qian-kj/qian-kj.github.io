Bio

I am a PhD student in the EPSRC CDT in Machine Learning Systems at the University of Edinburgh, supervised by Dr. Fengxiang He and Prof. Amos Storkey since September 2024. My research focuses on reinforcement learning agents, large language model agents, and interpreting their behaviour through the lens of rationality.
